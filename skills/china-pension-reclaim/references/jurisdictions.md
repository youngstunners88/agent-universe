# Jurisdiction reference

Verify against the counter before spending money. Practice moves faster than published guidance.

## When enrolment became compulsory for foreigners

| City | Compulsory from | Note |
|---|---|---|
| National floor | 15 Oct 2011 | MOHRSS Order No. 16 made participation compulsory nationwide. |
| **Shanghai** | **16 Aug 2021** | Shanghai ran a carve-out (沪人社养发〔2009〕38号) letting employers opt foreigners out, or enrol them in only 3 of the 5 funds by agreement. It expired 15 Aug 2021. **Most employers opted out.** This is the single most important date in a Shanghai claim. |
| Beijing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou | 2011 | Enforced from the national date; enrolment much more likely. |

An enrolment window is therefore `max(employment start, city compulsory date) → employment end`,
unless the person can show a payslip with a social-insurance deduction before that date.

### The 双减 overlap

The July 2021 "double reduction" policy shut down the private tutoring sector weeks before
Shanghai's carve-out expired. ESL teachers at training centres often have an enrolment window of
zero to a few months, and a dissolved employer. Both facts are survivable; the short window is
what caps the money.

## Social-security treaty countries

Nationals of these countries may have been lawfully exempt from Chinese pension contributions
while remaining covered at home — so there may be no personal account to claim.

In force: **Germany, South Korea, Denmark, Canada, Finland, Switzerland, Netherlands, Spain,
Luxembourg, Japan, Serbia, Kyrgyzstan** (in force 14 Oct 2025).
Signed, not yet in force: **France**.

Not covered — compulsorily enrolled, no exemption available: **South Africa**, UK, US, Australia,
Ireland, New Zealand, and everyone else.

Exemptions generally cover pension and sometimes unemployment insurance only; medical, work-injury
and maternity are not exempted under most agreements. An employer who enrolled an exempt person
anyway still created a real account, and the balance is still claimable.

## Shanghai counter facts

- Service item: 外籍、台港澳居民养老保险个人账户终止 (termination of the pension personal account
  for foreign / HK / Macau / Taiwan residents).
- Eligibility: male under 60 or female under 55, employment ended **and** departed China; or death,
  claimed by heirs.
- Where: the 社保分中心 (district branch) of last enrolment, or a 社区事务受理服务中心.
- Counter hours: weekdays 09:00–11:30 and 13:30–16:30.
- Time limit: **20 working days** to review, action and report the result.
- Fee: **不收费** — none.
- Channel: **window only.** Not available online, by post, by app, or at a self-service terminal.
- Agent: permitted, with the principal's signed 授权委托书 plus the agent's **original PRC resident
  ID card and copies of both sides**.
- Translation: any non-Chinese document needs a Chinese translation bearing a translation agency's
  chop.

## Document authentication

China acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention effective **7 Nov 2023**; Chinese embassies ceased
legalisation on that date. A power of attorney is a private document — notarise first to make it a
public document, then apostille.

| Country | Apostille authority |
|---|---|
| South Africa | DIRCO Legalisation Section, Pretoria (High Court for some document types) |
| UK | FCDO Legalisation Office |
| US | Secretary of State of the issuing state |
| Australia | DFAT |

## Adjacent money worth asking about

- **住房公积金 housing fund** — a separate institution, separate claim. Voluntary for foreigners in
  Shanghai, but where paid it is often larger than the pension account.
- **Medical personal account** — liquidated at the medical insurance office, not the social
  insurance branch. The agent is already in the building; do both in one trip.
- **Unpaid wages or severance** from a dissolved employer — a labour arbitration matter, separate
  from this, and subject to a one-year limitation period that has usually expired.
