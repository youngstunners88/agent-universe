Unclaimed in China

If you worked in China on a work permit and left without closing your accounts, the money taken off your payslip is still sitting there in your name — the 8% pension, the 2% medical account, and, if your employer paid it, a housing fund where both shares are yours. None of it expires. Most people never find out.

待办PENDING
The short answer 结论

Yes — but two things decide it, and neither is the paperwork.

Whether an account was ever opened, and whether you can still reach a Chinese bank account to be paid into. The worked example is a South African ESL teacher: Shanghai from July 2018, left the country August 2022, stayed on the payroll remotely until February 2023, ¥16,000 a month, employer since dissolved, no termination letter. Change the inputs and the whole kit rewrites itself.

Can he claim from abroad?
Yes
Leaving China permanently is itself the qualifying event. No treaty needed — and South Africa has none anyway.
Must he fly to Shanghai?
Not to file
An agent with a PRC ID card files for him. Collecting is the harder half — see the payout question in the estimator.
What does the state charge?
Nothing
不收费 at every counter. The cost is notary, apostille and translation.
Is it too late?
No
The personal account has no claim deadline. Under-declaration complaints do — those expire in two years.

Step 00 · What is in there 个人账户储存额估算

Estimate the balance before you spend a cent on paperwork

Three separate pots, with different rules. Pension and medical personal accounts hold your contributions only — the employer's 16% pension share goes to the city pooling fund and is gone. The housing fund is the opposite and the reason to check: both your share and your employer's are yours, and it is often the largest of the three.

参保情况Your enrolment
Identity 身份信息
The record is keyed to the passport number on file when you enrolled. If yours has been renewed since, the operator searching the new number finds nothing — which reads exactly like never having been enrolled.
Dates and pay 参保期间
Pay year by year 各年月薪

Contributions are calculated on what you earned at the time, so a rising salary matters. Edit any year directly, or set a starting figure and a raise above and apply it. Years outside your contributing window are dimmed — they are kept in case the window turns out to be longer than assumed.

Housing fund 住房公积金
How you would be paid 收款账户 Banks restrict foreigners' accounts when the residence permit on file expires, so "it existed in 2022" is not the same as "it works". Test it before you spend anything on a notary.
Where you are now 现居地 This decides your notary and apostille chain, not whether you can claim — the claim itself is unaffected by where you live. It also decides how easy the phone call is, and how cheaply you could go in person if the payout account turns out to be the blocker.

Step 01 · The phone call 先打电话

What the call can and cannot get you

Be realistic about this, because it is where most people quit. 12333 will not read personal account data to an unverified caller, and the English desk on 12345 mostly opens a ticket. What the call reliably gets you is three things: whether a record exists, which district branch holds it, and the payout-account rule. The balance itself usually comes from the online account or the counter. A refusal to give you a figure is not evidence that no account exists.

Get your own eyes on the record instead

Free电子社保卡

More reliable than the hotline, and it works from anywhere. Register on the national portal si.12333.gov.cn, or open the 电子社保卡 mini-programme inside WeChat or Alipay, choosing 证件类型 → 外国护照.   • The catch: registration usually sends an SMS code to a Chinese mobile number. If yours is dead, this is a job for your agent, or for a friend who can receive the code.
  • What you are looking for: the months listed, the declared base for each, and the account status — 封存 or 中断 is what you want to see.

    Vetting whoever you pick
    
  
      
    
    • Verify the company exists. Ask for the 营业执照 (business licence) and check the registration on gsxt.gov.cn, the national enterprise credit system. A firm that will not show you a licence number is not a firm.
      • 
    
    • Get the scope in writing — specifically whether it covers the medical account and the housing fund, which are separate offices and the usual hidden extras.
      • 
    
    • A success fee is fine. Routing the money is not. Being paid only if the claim works is a normal arrangement. The payout arriving in their account is not — it defaults to an account in your own name, and anyone proposing otherwise is either mistaken about the procedure or not planning to follow it.
      • 
    
    • Walk away from: a guaranteed figure quoted before anyone has seen your record; pressure to notarise before the document list is confirmed; a percentage of the payout rather than a flat or fixed success fee; any reluctance to put the fee in writing.
      • 
    
    • Also worth asking: your country's chamber of commerce in Shanghai, and long-running expat community groups — both outlast any individual agency.

    Read before you start 风险提示
    
  
    What actually derails these claims
    
  
    
    
    You can file but cannot collect
    The most common ending. Payment defaults to a Chinese account in your own name — never your agent's. If yours is closed or restricted, reopening it usually needs you there in person. Some branches will pay an account named in a notarised power of attorney, which is why the template below has a payee clause. Settle this on the first call, before you spend anything.
    
    
    Arrears freeze everything
    A termination cannot be processed while the account still reads 正常参保 or shows 欠费. If your vanished employer never filed the departure record, or declared months it never paid, the counter will say 先处理欠费 — clear the arrears first. That needs the company's deregistration record, which your agent requests at the counter.
    
    
    Withdrawing is one-way
    It zeroes your contribution history. Fifteen years of contributions is what unlocks an actual monthly pension for life — if you are anywhere near that, or might work in China again, leaving the account dormant and earning interest may be worth far more than the lump sum.
    
    
    Never pay an agent from the payout
    The money cannot legitimately land in an agent's account. Anyone who proposes collecting it for you and forwarding your share is describing something the counter will not do. Agree a flat fee, paid separately.
    
    
    The dissolved employer is not fatal
    When a company deregisters, its social-insurance registration closes and your status drops to terminated — which is the proof the counter wants. Your exit stamps and expired work permit corroborate it. Bring the statement of circumstances and let the branch check the registry.
    
    
    Complaints expire, the account does not
    The balance itself has no deadline. But if your employer under-declared your base or never enrolled you at all, the window to make that a complaint to the labour authorities is generally two years from when you knew — long gone for most people reading this. Claim what is in the account; do not count on recovering what should have been.
